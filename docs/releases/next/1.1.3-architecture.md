## How ratings work

Scores are authored editorial suitability, not benchmark results, popularity statistics or an official Go ranking. Compare three explicit scenarios: a small tool/CLI, a domain-rich application, and independently deployed distributed services. 1 means usually avoid, 3 means useful with a specific need, and 5 means a strong starting choice. Implementation and operation costs are shown separately. Styles are not mutually exclusive; DDD and CQRS describe different decisions from deployment topology.

The architecture unit has a scenario picker and a sorted comparison linked to each lesson. Each design lesson includes a flow diagram, useful cases, cautions, a highlighted complete program, a walkthrough, a simpler alternative and primary references. Every reading contributes a matching pair and a three-answer mistake-review question. Each of the 53 new lessons has its own concept tag, so learning Strategy does not unlock an unstudied Visitor pair or mark every behavioral pattern as the same mistake.

## Implementation and release boundary

- New material stays bundled and available offline; it does not require executing native network services or cloud commands in the iOS sandbox.
- Existing reading IDs and course progress are preserved. The original three architecture lessons receive diagrams without changing their IDs.
- Classic inheritance-based patterns are explicitly described as composition-based Go adaptations where appropriate. State also teaches when an enum and transition methods are simpler than separate state objects.
- Single-owner examples, in-memory queues and deduplication maps identify their durability and concurrency limits. Production retries need capped backoff, jitter, idempotency and one total deadline.
- The public website contains the full expanded course. It distinguishes the public App Store version from this submitted update.
- App and share extension: 1.1.3 (37). Archive/export validation passed, build 37 is VALID and available to Internal QA, and the two-item review submission is WAITING_FOR_REVIEW. Release is AFTER_APPROVAL.
