## Changes

- Duo workspace and Projects exit while the laptop keyboard is open.
- Topic-specific vector diagrams for all 69 lessons and prediction challenges.
- Expanded practical explanations, pitfalls and official references.
- Corrections to 21 Go explanations, including slice aliasing, named results,
  closures, deferred arguments, type sets and channel ownership.
- Consistent Go highlighting in prose, quizzes, matching drills and examples.
- Page-level scrolling for embedded lesson source; internal scrolling in full screen.
- Canonical website support/privacy URLs and Terms of Use in Settings.
