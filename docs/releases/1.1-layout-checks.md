They do not use the forced laptop test layout. The no-keyboard check establishes
the hidden-keyboard layout; it is not a claim that a physical keyboard was tested.

ordinary-phone runtime. The exact reported sequence passed on the listed
simulator systems; a physical-device verification is not claimed.

Local result bundles are retained under `build/release-1.1`: `final20-iphone13-all-three`,
`final20-iphone17`, `final20-ipad`, `final20-duo-laptop`, and `native20-header-safe-band`.
The final header tint change is visual only; the interaction checks preceded it.

The final ordinary landscape capture also passed, with native frames for Files
open, Files closed and a hittable software keyboard. Its result is
`native-iphone13-landscape-default-final20`. All three 2532 × 1170 images were
visually inspected and saved under `docs/screenshots/iphone-landscape-*.png`.

XCTest's rotated or inactive-display images are rejected. Native screenshots use
the display UUID returned by public `simctl io enumerate` during a verified
XCTest capture hold. Ordinary single-display devices use simctl's default LCD,
because display-port UUIDs can change when the automation session starts.
`scripts/capture_workspace_frames.py` retains the capture
log and result bundle beside each group of frames for inspection.
